Routes

Map operations

System-level — affects everyone: Rebuild all tiles regenerates the entire tile archive (emergency only); Back up snapshots the nonrecoverable admin/user data.

    Identity

    Short name is the route badge (e.g. 40). Long name is the full destination description (e.g. Northgate – Rainier Beach). Name is the fallback shown when the others are missing.

    Merge routes

    Combine routes that are the same line counted separately (e.g. two directions). Names join with "/" and identical stops merge into one.
